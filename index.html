Flagship innovation

Transforming Emergency Healthcare Through Intelligent Technology

Emergency healthcare transportation in India faces challenges arising from fragmented ambulance networks, limited real-time fleet visibility, patient location identification and inadequate coordination between ambulance operators and healthcare institutions.

RakshaRath™ is designed to address these challenges through a unified digital platform that brings ambulance booking, intelligent dispatch, GPS-enabled tracking, fleet monitoring and emergency healthcare coordination into an integrated ecosystem.

The platform is designed to support multiple ambulance categories, patient-condition-based vehicle selection, hospital capability mapping, patient referral coordination and integration with existing emergency healthcare infrastructure.

Pilot statusRakshaRath™ has been piloted in a private hospital in Jharkhand, providing practical insights into operational requirements and future product development. SilicoSoft provides the underlying technology platform and is not an ambulance service operator.

About SilicoSoft

Innovation built in India.
Designed for real-world impact.

SilicoSoft Technologies Private Limited was established in March 2026 with a vision to develop innovative, intelligent and scalable technology solutions addressing real-world challenges across healthcare, education, enterprise management and digital infrastructure.

The company focuses on indigenous software product development, artificial intelligence, machine learning, intelligent automation, cloud computing, data analytics and system integration.

Through its portfolio of domain-specific software products, SilicoSoft aims to improve accessibility, operational efficiency, resource utilization and data-driven decision-making across public and private sector organizations.

As part of its long-term research and development strategy, SilicoSoft envisions expanding into AI computing infrastructure, data centres, embedded systems, electronics and semiconductor design. These are future roadmap areas and are not presented as currently operational facilities.
