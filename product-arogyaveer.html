Problem addressed

Healthcare organizations often manage professional contact databases and outreach through fragmented spreadsheets, unstructured lists and broad communications.

Proposed solution

ArogyaVeer™ provides structured professional records, geographical and professional segmentation, targeted outreach and controlled digital communication workflows.

Innovation & differentiation

Combines multi-dimensional audience segmentation with communication workflow controls so outreach can be targeted by location, qualification, specialization and related attributes.

Target users

Healthcare organizations, professional outreach teams, institutional engagement teams and authorized healthcare communication programs.
