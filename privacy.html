Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SilicoSoft Technologies Private Limited (“SilicoSoft”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal data when you use silicosoft.in and SilicoSoft applications and services.

This policy applies to ArogyaVeer™, Raksharath™, Asarfi HRMS™, SilicoMed™, HRMS System, HIMS System, CRM Apps, and other SilicoSoft web, mobile and enterprise applications.

The exact data processed by each app depends on the functions actually enabled, your organisation’s configuration, your role and the permissions you grant. An app should not collect a category of personal data merely because that category is listed in this policy.

For data collected directly by SilicoSoft for our own purposes, such as website enquiries, support, security and service administration, SilicoSoft may determine why and how that information is processed.

3. App-specific notices

ArogyaVeer™

ArogyaVeer is covered by this policy. Its actual collection, use, sharing and permission requirements depend on the features enabled in the production build. Any health, location, camera, media, notification or other sensitive permission must be disclosed at the point of collection and in the relevant App Store/Google Play privacy declaration when used.

Raksharath™

Raksharath is covered by this policy. Only data required for enabled functionality should be collected. Any sensitive or restricted permission used by the production build must be accompanied by an appropriate in-app disclosure, purpose statement and store declaration.

Asarfi HRMS™

Asarfi HRMS may process employee identity, employment, department/designation, attendance, roster, leave, payroll-related, work-allocation, document, authentication and technical information where those functions are enabled by the subscribing organisation.

SilicoMed™

SilicoMed may process healthcare-related identity, registration, appointment, diagnostic, clinical, document, authentication and technical information where those functions are enabled. Health information should be handled as sensitive user data and only for the healthcare/service purpose for which it is provided.

HRMS System

HRMS System may process employee profile, attendance, leave, roster, payroll-related, work-allocation, performance, documents, authentication and technical information where enabled for the customer organisation.

HIMS System

HIMS System may process patient registration, appointments, clinical and diagnostic information, billing/operational records, user authentication, audit and technical information where enabled for a hospital or healthcare organisation.

CRM Apps

CRM Apps may process business-contact, lead/customer, enquiry, follow-up, assignment, notes, communication and technical information where enabled. Personal data should be used only for the relevant business relationship, service or support purpose.

These app-specific summaries are intentionally limited to the categories reasonably associated with the stated product functions. Store disclosures and in-app notices must be checked against the actual production code, SDKs, permissions and integrations before each release.

4. How we collect data

Information may be provided by you, provided or authorised by your organisation, generated through use of the Services, received from integrated systems, or collected through device features and permissions you enable.

5. How we use personal data

  • Provide, configure, operate and support the Services.
  • Create and administer accounts and authenticate users.
  • Perform healthcare, HRMS, HIMS, CRM or other enabled enterprise workflows.
  • Maintain security, detect misuse, troubleshoot errors and keep audit records.
  • Communicate about support, service requests and operational matters.
  • Improve service reliability, performance and usability.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory and contractual requirements.

We do not sell personal data.

6. Consent, notices and device permissions

Where consent is required, the notice should clearly identify the data requested and the specific purpose before collection. Withdrawal of consent should be reasonably accessible. Device permissions such as camera, photos, notifications or location can be managed through device settings, but disabling a permission may prevent a feature that depends on it from working.

7. Sharing and service providers

We may share data only as reasonably necessary with your organisation, authorised hosting/cloud/database providers, email or notification providers, analytics or monitoring services, security providers, integrations enabled for your organisation, professional advisers or authorities where required by law.

Third-party processors and SDKs used by an app must be included in that app’s actual privacy and store disclosures where they access or process user data.

8. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, customer instruction, audit requirement, dispute resolution or legal/regulatory obligation. Retention periods may differ for healthcare, employment, payroll and other regulated records. Data no longer required should be deleted, anonymised or securely disposed of, subject to lawful retention requirements and backup cycles.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal data, including access controls, authentication, role-based permissions, monitoring, logging, backup controls and secure communications appropriate to the Service and risk.

10. Your rights and privacy requests

Subject to applicable law and the role SilicoSoft plays for the relevant data, you may request information about your personal data, correction or updating, deletion or erasure where applicable, withdrawal of consent and grievance redressal. Where a subscribing organisation controls the data, we may coordinate the request with that organisation.

11. Account and data deletion

If a SilicoSoft Service allows account creation, you may request deletion of the account and associated personal data through an in-app deletion option where available or through our Privacy & Data Deletion Request page. Some data may be retained where required by law or legitimate audit/security requirements.

12. Children and minors

Where a Service is used in a context involving children or minors, the responsible institution or adult must use the Service in accordance with applicable law and obtain any required consent or authorisation. SilicoSoft does not knowingly use children’s personal data for behavioural advertising.

13. Website forms and cookies

silicosoft.in may use technical services required for hosting, form delivery, fonts, analytics or business communication. Information submitted through the enquiry form is used to respond to your request. If non-essential analytics or advertising cookies are introduced, the website should provide an appropriate notice and consent mechanism before such cookies are used where required.

14. Digital Personal Data Protection framework

SilicoSoft is aligning its products and processes with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 as applicable provisions come into force, including clear notices, purpose limitation, consent/withdrawal mechanisms where applicable, reasonable safeguards, grievance handling and processes for applicable Data Principal rights.

15. App-store disclosures

Each app’s Apple App Store privacy disclosures and Google Play Data Safety declarations must reflect the actual production build, including SDKs, permissions, integrations, collection, sharing and deletion practices. This website policy is not a substitute for completing those app-specific declarations accurately.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our Services, legal obligations or processing practices change. The current version will be published here with an updated effective date.
