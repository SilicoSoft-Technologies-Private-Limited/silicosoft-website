Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SilicoSoft Technologies Private Limited (“SilicoSoft”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal data when you use silicosoft.in, our mobile applications, web applications, enterprise platforms, support channels and related digital services (collectively, the “Services”).

Our Services include healthcare, diagnostics, workforce and HR management, education, enterprise operations, integrations and custom software. The exact data processed depends on the product, features enabled by your organisation, your role and the permissions you grant.

1. Our role

For information collected directly by SilicoSoft for our own purposes, such as website enquiries, account administration, support, security and service communications, SilicoSoft may determine why and how that information is processed.

For many enterprise deployments, your employer, hospital, laboratory, educational institution or subscribing organisation determines the purposes for which your information is processed. In such cases, SilicoSoft generally processes data on that organisation’s instructions to provide and support the Services.

3. How we collect data

Information may be provided directly by you, provided or authorised by your organisation, generated through your use of the Services, received from integrated systems, or collected through device features and permissions you enable.

4. How we use personal data

  • Provide, configure, operate and support the Services.
  • Create and administer accounts and authenticate users.
  • Perform healthcare, diagnostic, HR, payroll, attendance, education or enterprise workflows where applicable.
  • Maintain security, detect misuse, troubleshoot errors and keep audit records.
  • Communicate about support, service requests and operational matters.
  • Improve service reliability, performance and usability.
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory and contractual requirements.

We do not sell personal data.

5. Consent and permissions

Where processing is based on consent, we aim to provide a clear description of the information requested and the purpose for which it is needed. You may withdraw consent where applicable through the relevant app, device settings or by contacting us. Mobile permissions such as camera, photos, notifications or location can also be managed through your device settings.

6. Sharing and service providers

We may share data only as reasonably necessary with your organisation, hosting/cloud/database providers, email or notification providers, analytics or monitoring services, security providers, integrations specifically enabled for your organisation, professional advisers or authorities where required by law.

Service providers handling personal data on our behalf are expected to process it only for authorised purposes and apply appropriate safeguards.

7. Retention

We retain personal data only as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, service, customer instruction, audit requirement, dispute resolution or legal/regulatory obligation. Retention periods may differ for healthcare, employment, payroll, education and other regulated records. Where data is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it, subject to lawful retention requirements and backup cycles.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal data, including access controls, authentication, role-based permissions, monitoring, logging, backup controls and secure communications appropriate to the Service and risk.

9. Your rights and privacy requests

Subject to applicable law and the role SilicoSoft plays for the relevant data, you may request access or information about your personal data, correction or updating, deletion or erasure where applicable, withdrawal of consent and grievance redressal.

If your data is controlled by a subscribing organisation, we may coordinate the request with that organisation.

10. Account and data deletion

If a SilicoSoft Service allows account creation, you may request deletion of the account and associated personal data through any in-app deletion option made available for that Service or through our Privacy & Data Deletion Request page. Some information may be retained where required by law, for legitimate security/audit purposes, or where controlled by the subscribing organisation.

11. Children and minors

Some education or healthcare Services may operate in contexts involving children. The relevant institution or responsible adult is expected to use the Service in accordance with applicable law and obtain any required consent or authorisation. SilicoSoft does not knowingly use children’s personal data for behavioural advertising.

12. International processing

Depending on deployment, customer instructions, integrations and service providers, information may be processed or stored in India or other permitted locations. Any cross-border processing is handled subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements.

13. Website forms and external services

Our website may use service providers necessary for hosting, form delivery, fonts, analytics or business communication. Information submitted through the enquiry form is used to respond to your request and may be processed by the service used to deliver that form.

14. Digital Personal Data Protection framework

SilicoSoft is aligning its products and processes with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 as their applicable provisions come into force, including clear notices, purpose limitation, consent and withdrawal mechanisms where applicable, reasonable safeguards, grievance handling and processes for applicable data-principal rights.

15. App-store disclosures

Each SilicoSoft app’s Apple App Store privacy disclosures and Google Play Data Safety declarations should reflect the actual practices of that specific app, including third-party SDKs, integrations and permissions. These declarations may differ between apps because features differ.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our Services, legal obligations or processing practices change. The current version will be published here with an updated effective date.
