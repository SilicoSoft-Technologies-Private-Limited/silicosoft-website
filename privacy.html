Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SilicoSoft Technologies Private Limited (“SilicoSoft”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal data when you use silicosoft.in and our software products and services.

Our current product portfolio covered by this policy includes Education Management Software, SilicoMed™ — Hospital Management System, ArogyaVeer™ — Marketing CRM Management, Asarfi HRMS™ — Human Resources Management, and RakshaRath™ — Ambulance Management.

The exact data processed depends on the functions enabled by the subscribing organisation, the user's role, integrations and permissions granted. Listing a data category here does not mean every product collects that category.

1. Our role

For data collected directly by SilicoSoft for our own purposes, such as website enquiries, customer support, security and service administration, SilicoSoft may determine why and how that information is processed.

For enterprise deployments, the subscribing hospital, employer, educational institution or other customer generally determines the business purpose of processing. In those cases, SilicoSoft normally processes data on that organisation's instructions as its technology/service provider.

3. Product-specific notices

Education Management Software

May process student admission and profile information, attendance, academics, examinations, fees, faculty/staff information and related institutional records where those modules are enabled.

SilicoMed™ — Hospital Management System

May process patient registration, appointment, clinical, diagnostic, pharmacy, billing, insurance/TPA, discharge, document, authentication and operational information where those functions are enabled. Health information must be handled only for authorised healthcare and related operational purposes.

ArogyaVeer™ — Marketing CRM Management

May process leads, enquiries, campaign/source information, contact information, service interests, follow-ups, assignments, feedback and communication history for authorised marketing, outreach and relationship-management workflows.

Asarfi HRMS™ — Human Resources Management

May process employee identity, employment, department/designation, attendance, roster, leave, payroll-related records, work allocation, documents, performance, authentication and technical information. If the product is renamed in the future, this policy will continue to apply to its successor or renamed HRMS product unless a replacement policy states otherwise.

RakshaRath™ — Ambulance Management

Designed to connect patients, ambulances and hospitals. It may process patient/contact details, ambulance requests, pickup/destination details, ambulance and crew assignment, trip status, timestamps, emergency information and location data where necessary for dispatch, routing, tracking or hospital coordination.

4. How we use personal data

  • Provide, configure, operate and support the products.
  • Authenticate users and administer accounts.
  • Perform hospital, education, HR, CRM and ambulance workflows enabled by the customer.
  • Maintain security, audit trails, troubleshooting and service reliability.
  • Provide customer support and operational communications.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory and contractual requirements.

We do not sell personal data.

5. Consent and device permissions

Where consent is required, the relevant notice should identify the data requested and the purpose before collection. Device permissions such as camera, files, notifications or location should be requested only where a product feature requires them and can be controlled through device settings.

6. Sharing and service providers

We may process or share information only as reasonably necessary with the subscribing organisation, authorised hosting/cloud/database providers, messaging providers, security providers, integrations enabled by the customer, professional advisers or authorities where required by law. Third-party SDKs and processors used in a production app must be reflected in the relevant app-store disclosures.

7. Retention and deletion

Retention depends on customer instructions, healthcare/employment/education obligations, audit requirements, legal obligations and backup cycles. Where deletion is permitted, data should be deleted, anonymised or securely disposed of when no longer required.

Account or data-deletion requests may be submitted through an in-app deletion mechanism where available or through our Privacy & Data Deletion Request page.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards including authentication, access controls, role-based permissions, audit logs, monitoring, backups and secure communications appropriate to the service and risk.

9. Children and minors

Education and healthcare products may be used in contexts involving children or minors. The responsible institution or lawful guardian must provide any consent or authorisation required by applicable law. SilicoSoft does not knowingly use children's personal data for behavioural advertising.

10. Digital Personal Data Protection framework

SilicoSoft is aligning its products and processes with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 as applicable provisions come into force, including purpose-specific notices, consent/withdrawal mechanisms where applicable, reasonable safeguards, grievance handling and applicable Data Principal rights.

11. App-store disclosures

Apple App Store privacy disclosures and Google Play Data Safety declarations must match each actual production build, including permissions, SDKs, integrations, data collection, data sharing and deletion practices.
