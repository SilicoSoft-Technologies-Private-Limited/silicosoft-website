Privacy & Data Deletion Requests

This page allows users of SilicoSoft Technologies Private Limited applications and services to request access, correction, deletion or other privacy assistance.

How to request account or data deletion

  1. If the app provides an in-app deletion option, use that option first.
  2. If you cannot access the app, email hello@silicosoft.in.
  3. Include the app/service name, your organisation if applicable, and the email address or mobile number linked to your account.
  4. Do not send passwords, OTPs, full medical records or unnecessary sensitive information.

Suggested email subject: Account or Data Deletion Request

Email: hello@silicosoft.in

What happens next

We may need to verify your identity and determine whether SilicoSoft or your subscribing organisation controls the relevant data. Where your employer, hospital, laboratory, educational institution or other organisation controls the data, we may coordinate the request with that organisation.

Where deletion is permitted, we will delete or anonymise applicable account data and associated personal data within a reasonable period. Certain records may need to be retained for legal, regulatory, healthcare, employment, payroll, security, fraud-prevention, audit or dispute-resolution purposes. If retention is required, access will be limited to the purpose for which retention remains necessary.

Other privacy requests

You may also use the same email address to request correction of inaccurate information, withdrawal of consent where applicable, information about the handling of your personal data, or grievance redressal.
