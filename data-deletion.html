How to request deletion

  1. If the relevant app provides an in-app account deletion option, use that option first.
  2. Otherwise email hello@silicosoft.in.
  3. State the exact app name, your organisation if applicable, and the email address or mobile number linked to the account.
  4. State whether you want account deletion, deletion of particular personal data, correction, consent withdrawal, or another privacy request.
  5. Do not send passwords, OTPs, full medical records or unnecessary sensitive information.

Suggested subject: Account or Data Deletion Request

Email: hello@silicosoft.in

Verification and response

We may need to verify your identity and determine whether SilicoSoft or your subscribing organisation controls the relevant data. Where an employer, hospital, clinic, laboratory or other organisation controls the data, we may coordinate the request with that organisation.

Where deletion is permitted, applicable account data and personal data will be deleted or anonymised within a reasonable period. Certain records may need to be retained for legal, regulatory, healthcare, employment, payroll, security, fraud-prevention, audit or dispute-resolution purposes.

Important for app-store account deletion

If an app allows users to create an account directly, SilicoSoft should also provide an accessible way to initiate account deletion from inside the app where required by the applicable app-store rules. This webpage provides the public external deletion route and should be linked from the relevant store listing and app privacy settings where appropriate.

Other privacy requests

You may use the same email address to request correction of inaccurate information, withdrawal of consent where applicable, information about personal-data handling, or grievance redressal.
