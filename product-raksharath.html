Problem addressed

Emergency healthcare transportation can be fragmented across ambulance operators, dispatch teams and healthcare facilities, limiting real-time visibility and coordinated patient movement.

Proposed solution

RakshaRath™ provides a unified technology platform for ambulance booking, intelligent dispatch, GPS-enabled tracking, fleet monitoring and emergency healthcare coordination.

Innovation & differentiation

Location-aware dispatch, ambulance-category matching, hospital capability mapping and integration-oriented architecture designed for coordinated emergency transport operations.

Target users

Hospitals, ambulance operators, emergency response networks, healthcare institutions and government emergency-transport programs.

Development / deployment status

Pilot completed in a private hospital in Jharkhand. Product development continues based on operational learning. SilicoSoft provides the technology platform and is not an ambulance service operator.
